Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about installing and deploying Valenixia POS.

Does Valenixia POS require an active internet connection to ring up sales?
No. Valenixia is built offline-first. Every sale, inventory change, and customer record is committed instantly to your device's local encrypted storage and syncs automatically whenever a network connection is available.
How do I install the APK on an Android POS terminal or phone?
Download the APK file directly from this page, tap the download notification on your device, and allow "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted. The app installs within 10 seconds.
Can multiple cashiers work on different devices at the same store?
Yes. You can run one master register on Windows or Android and pair additional cashiers and mobile order-takers over local Wi-Fi or cloud sync without data conflicts.
How do I upgrade or manage my store subscription?
Subscription plans, add-ons (FBR Fiscal, Multi-Store HQ, WhatsApp Receipts), and license upgrades are managed directly within the app inside the Subscription & Billing Vault.
